# Asset credits

The v1.0 visual remaster uses a single Kenney art family for its interface, characters, enemies, pickups, and combat effects.

| Use | Pack | Creator | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buttons, panels, bars, slots | Kenney UI Pack 2.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack |
| Player, enemy, chest, XP sprites | Kenney Tiny Dungeon 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon |
| Hit, critical, death, level, boss, ultimate, pickup, mission effects | Kenney Particle Pack 1.1 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack |

Attribution is not required by CC0, but is included here to make the origin and reuse terms easy to audit.

- The application icon remains an original SVG in this repository.
- Projectiles, arena telegraphs, and a few continuous combat indicators remain Phaser primitives.
- Short sound effects remain synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.

The exact imported files and their renamed project paths are recorded in [`ASSET_MANIFEST.csv`](ASSET_MANIFEST.csv).
