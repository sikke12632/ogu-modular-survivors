# Asset credits

The final v1.0 school remaster uses a coordinated pixel-art set: MetroCity's
32 px students and school facade, Kenney Tiny Dungeon's 16 px monsters and
props, Kenney Pixel Adventure UI, and Kenney Particle effects. Assets are
displayed at integer-like scale with nearest-neighbor filtering so their
outlines and pixel density remain visually consistent.

| Use | Pack | Creator | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Student sprites and directional walk frames | MetroCity - Free Top Down Character Pack | JIK-A-4 | CC0 1.0 | https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack |
| School facade | MetroCity - Free External Top Down Environment Asset Pack | JIK-A-4 | CC0 1.0 | https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metro |
| Buttons, panels, bars, slots | Kenney UI Pack - Pixel Adventure | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-pixel-adventure |
| Monsters, props, chest, XP sprites | Kenney Tiny Dungeon 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon |
| Hit, critical, death, level, boss, ultimate, pickup, mission effects | Kenney Particle Pack 1.1 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack |

Attribution is not required by CC0, but is included here to make the origin and reuse terms easy to audit.

- The application icon remains an original SVG in this repository.
- School-supply projectiles, the schoolyard, arena telegraphs, and continuous
  combat indicators are original Phaser-drawn pixel graphics.
- Short sound effects remain synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.

The exact imported files and their renamed project paths are recorded in [`ASSET_MANIFEST.csv`](ASSET_MANIFEST.csv).
